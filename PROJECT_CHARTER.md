# MyGolfBuddy Project Charter

## Product Name

MyGolfBuddy

## Product Vision

MyGolfBuddy helps golfers answer a simple question:

"Can I play golf right now?"

The application analyzes current weather conditions, nearby golf courses, and eventually real-time tee-time availability to provide actionable recommendations.

---

## Mission Statement

Reduce the time required for a golfer to decide whether to play golf from multiple websites and apps to a single recommendation screen.

---

## Core User Scenario

A golfer suddenly has 1 to 3 hours available.

The golfer opens MyGolfBuddy and immediately receives:

- Current golf suitability score
- Weather conditions
- Nearby golf courses
- Available tee times (future release)
- Recommended course

---

## Target Audience

Primary Audience:

- Casual golfers
- Weekend golfers
- Retired golfers
- Golf enthusiasts

Secondary Audience:

- Traveling golfers
- Business travelers

---

## Success Criteria

The user can:

1. Open the application.
2. Automatically detect current location.
3. View weather conditions.
4. View a golf suitability score.
5. Make a golf decision within 30 seconds.

---

## Product Principles

- Mobile-first
- Fast
- Simple
- Actionable
- Minimal user input
- Cloud-ready
- Azure-deployable

---

## MVP Objective

Provide weather-based golf recommendations using current location and weather conditions.

---

## Long-Term Objective

Become a personal golfing assistant capable of recommending where and when to play based on:

- Weather
- Travel time
- Tee-time availability
- User preferences
